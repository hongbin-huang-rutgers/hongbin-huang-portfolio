Partnership Development

Annual Sponsorship Acquisition Program

A structured outreach initiative that secured annual sponsorship funding for the Rutgers Chinese Students and Scholars Association.

Role

External Relations Department Director

Organization

Rutgers Chinese Students and Scholars Association

Skills

Negotiation, sponsor outreach, stakeholder management, professional communication

Challenge

The organization needed dependable external funding to support a full year of student programs. The work required identifying suitable partners, communicating the value of the student audience, and maintaining professional relationships through agreement and execution.

Approach

I led sponsor outreach and relationship development, coordinated discussions around partnership expectations, and organized the information needed to move opportunities from initial contact to confirmed support.

Outcome

The initiative secured the organization’s annual sponsorship funding. It also demonstrated how disciplined outreach, reliable follow-up, and clear stakeholder communication can convert external relationships into operational resources.

What I Learned

This experience strengthened my interest in supplier and partner relationship management, negotiation, and the coordination work that connects external resources with internal operational needs.
