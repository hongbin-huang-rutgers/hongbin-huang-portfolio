Sponsor Outreach and Resource Tracker

A shared tracking structure designed to make sponsor research, ownership, and follow-up more visible and accountable.

Role

Process designer

Context

External relations operations

Skills

Spreadsheet organization, workflow design, follow-up planning, resource tracking

Challenge

External relations work can become difficult to manage when sponsor information, social media sources, contacts, and follow-up dates are stored in different places or remembered informally.

Approach

I organized the workflow around clear fields for ownership, source channels, contact information, outreach status, recent follow-up, next action, and supporting evidence. This structure made it easier to understand the current state of each opportunity.

Outcome

The tracker created a shared operational view of sponsor development work, improving accountability and helping the team focus on the next required action instead of repeating research.

What I Learned

Simple information systems can create meaningful operational value. Clear ownership, standardized status fields, and scheduled follow-up are directly relevant to procurement, supplier management, and supply chain coordination.
